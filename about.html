About me

I study electrolyte chemistry and materials design for electrochemical energy storage — in particular, how solvation, phase behavior, and ion transport govern battery performance. I started in chemistry at the University of Illinois, where I first worked on redox flow batteries, and now study Materials Science & Engineering at Columbia University.

Away from the bench, I’m usually cooking.
