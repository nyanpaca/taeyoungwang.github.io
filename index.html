Taeyoung Wang

I’m a Materials Science & Engineering undergraduate at Columbia University (Class of 2027), researching battery electrolytes in Prof. Yuan Yang’s lab. I’m curious about how solvation, phase behavior, and ion transport decide whether a battery works — from gel polymer electrolytes in lithium-ion cells to room-temperature potassium–sulfur flow batteries.
