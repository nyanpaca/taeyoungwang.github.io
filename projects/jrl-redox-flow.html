Where it started

My first research experience was in Prof. Joaquín Rodríguez-López’s lab at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working on solid-state redox flow batteries and the automation of electrochemical experiments for high-throughput electrochemistry.

Outreach

At the Beckman Open House (2023–2024), I planned and presented an exhibition on electrochemistry and batteries, together with the lab’s electrochemistry robot.
