// Project 04 · Columbia

Solubility data
for ML screening

2025 —Ongoing

Question

Which solvents dissolve the most K₂S? Finding a good catholyte solvent for potassium–sulfur batteries by trial and error is slow. Machine-learning screening can narrow the search — but it needs reliable experimental data to learn from.

What I do

I measure K₂S solubility across individual solvents and solvent mixtures, providing the experimental data that the machine-learning screen uses to identify high-solubility candidates. The work builds directly on the titration methods from the K–S battery project.
