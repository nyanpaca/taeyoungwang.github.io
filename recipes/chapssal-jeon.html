Chapssal-jeon

Serves 210 min

Small, chewy glutinous rice pancakes, pan-fried until golden and served with a sweet syrup-and-sugar sauce.

Ingredients

  • Glutinous (sweet) rice flour
  • Water
  • Cooking oil

Sauce

  • Oligosaccharide syrup*
  • Sugar

Steps

  1. Take out a generous amount of glutinous rice flour.
  2. Add water bit by bit — stop while the dough still feels slightly dry.
  3. Mix with a spoon; once it mostly comes together, switch to your hands.
  4. Roll the dough into a long log, pinch off small pieces, roll each into a ball, then press it flat with your hand.
  5. Pour plenty of oil into a frying pan (a little less than you would for deep-frying) and drop in the pancakes.
  6. When they start to puff up, flip them. Once golden, take them out and blot the oil with a paper towel.

Sauce: oligosaccharide syrup with sugar.

*Substitutions

  • No oligosaccharide syrup? Use sugar or another syrup. Oligo syrup is milder, so start with less and adjust.
