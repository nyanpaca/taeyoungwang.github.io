Gyudon

Serves 220 min

Sweet-savory beef and onion simmered with soy sauce and mirin, finished with a softly set egg and a raw yolk on top of rice.

Ingredients

  • ½ onion
  • Eggs
  • Olive oil
  • Thinly sliced beef for shabu-shabu (any thinly sliced meat works!)
  • Enoki mushrooms (optional)
  • Soy sauce
  • Mirin
  • Oligosaccharide syrup (Korean rice syrup)
  • Cooked rice, to serve

Steps

  1. Slice half an onion — not too thin.
  2. Coat a frying pan with olive oil.
  3. Add the onion and sauté until translucent.
  4. Add the beef, plus enoki mushrooms if you like.
  5. Once the meat is mostly cooked, pour in some water (as much as you like).
  6. Season with soy sauce, mirin, and syrup in a 3 : 4 : 1 ratio, then adjust the rest of the seasoning with soy sauce.
  7. When it’s nearly done, pour a beaten egg over the top in a circle. Don’t stir — leave it alone until it’s mostly set.
  8. When everything is cooked, spoon it over rice and top with a separated egg yolk.
