Pork Galbi
Rice Bowl

Serves 240 min

Soy-garlic glazed pork over rice, with fresh perilla leaves and a fried egg on top.

Ingredients

  • Pork ribs (galbi) or pork collar
  • ½ onion
  • 1 spoon minced garlic
  • 3 spoons soy sauce
  • 2 spoons mirin (Korean matsul)*
  • Oligosaccharide syrup*
  • Black pepper
  • 3 spoons water
  • Sesame oil
  • Perilla leaves
  • Egg
  • Cooked rice

Steps

  1. Chop the onion and garlic into small pieces and add them to a frying pan.
  2. Blot the blood off the meat with a paper towel, score it with a knife, and add it to the pan.
  3. Add 3 spoons soy sauce, 1.5 spoons oligosaccharide syrup, 2 spoons mirin, a little pepper, and 1 spoon minced garlic.
  4. Massage the meat with your hands so it’s coated in the sauce, then add 3 spoons of water.
  5. Cook the meat over medium heat and cut it into pieces with scissors. Once the fat renders and the sauce has reduced, drizzle a round of sesame oil and add as much syrup as you like.
  6. Top the rice with the meat, perilla leaves, and a fried egg. For the perilla leaves: fold them and cut with scissors.

*Substitutions

  • No mirin? Use dry white wine with a little sugar, to taste.
  • No oligosaccharide syrup? Use sugar or another syrup. Oligo syrup is milder, so start with less and adjust.
