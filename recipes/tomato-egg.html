Tomato & Egg

Serves 215 min

Soft scrambled eggs simmered with sweet, saucy tomatoes, so the eggs soak up the tomato flavor.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 3 tomatoes
  • Butter
  • Water
  • Sugar
  • A little salt

Steps

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl and cut the tomatoes.
  2. Heat a pan and add butter.
  3. Pour in the eggs and stir with a spatula until scrambled. Set aside.
  4. In another pan, add the tomatoes, water, salt, and plenty of sugar.
  5. Cover with a lid and simmer until the tomatoes are soft.
  6. Add the scrambled eggs and simmer a little longer so they take on the tomato flavor.
